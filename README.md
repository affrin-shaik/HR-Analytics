# HR-Analytics
This project involves the development of a comprehensive HR Analytics Dashboard designed to help organizations monitor their workforce and identify factors contributing to employee attrition.
By analyzing key metrics such as attrition rate, employee satisfaction, and demographics, this dashboard provides actionable insights to improve retention strategies and optimize human resource management. 


Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard tracks several critical HR metrics to provide a high-level overview of the organization's health:


Total Employee Count: The overall size of the workforce. 




Attrition Count: The total number of employees who have left the organization. 


Attrition Rate: The percentage of employees leaving, calculated as SUM(Attrition Count) / SUM(Employee Count). 


Active Employees: The current number of employees remaining in the company. 


Average Age: Monitoring the demographic distribution of the workforce. 

Technical Features & Data Analysis

Calculated Fields: Developed custom Tableau calculations to derive critical business metrics, including the Attrition Rate and Active Employee count. 


Interactive Filtering: Implemented cross-filtering by Department, Education Field, and Gender to allow stakeholders to drill down into specific employee segments. 


Data Bining: Utilized data binning on Employee Age to create age bands, enabling better demographic trend analysis. 

Advanced Visualizations:


Attrition by Age Band & Gender: Visualizing which demographics are most at risk. 


Job Satisfaction Heatmap: Correlating job roles with satisfaction levels to pinpoint morale issues. 


Attrition by Department: Identifying specific areas within the company with higher turnover rates. 

Tools Used

Tableau Desktop: For data visualization and dashboard construction. 


Microsoft Excel: Used as the primary data source for HR records. 


Data Modeling: Established a robust internal data model within Tableau to handle 1,400+ employee records efficiently. 


Key Insights

Departmental Impact: Identification of specific departments with attrition rates significantly higher than the company average. 


Retention Drivers: Analysis of how factors like Distance From Home, Monthly Income, and Percent Salary Hike correlate with an employee’s decision to stay or leave. 




Satisfaction Trends: Clear visibility into the relationship between Environment Satisfaction and long-term employee retention.
